# CommonGround — Overview

**One-liner.** A transparency-first civic finance platform: members pay one recurring subscription, contributions are pooled and allocated to vetted NGO initiatives, and every cent is auditable end-to-end.

## Mission
Make long-horizon social impact (restoration, education, community resilience) fundable with the predictability of SaaS and the auditability of a public ledger.

## Audiences
- **Members** — individuals subscribing monthly, allocating across initiatives, viewing receipts and impact.
- **NGOs / Initiative Managers** — organizations that publish initiatives, post reports, and receive monthly disbursements.
- **Admins** — platform staff vetting NGOs, monitoring treasury, resolving disputes, governing the system.
- **Public / Guests** — read-only transparency: how much was raised, where it went, what changed.

## Value proposition
1. **One subscription, many causes** — no donor fatigue.
2. **Transparent allocation** — the math is public, every payment broken down (fees, reserve, impact pool, distributable).
3. **Predictable funding for NGOs** — recurring revenue instead of one-off campaigns.
4. **Auditability by design** — double-entry ledger, immutable webhook log, audit trail.

## Non-goals (v1)
- Mobile app, peer-to-peer giving, multi-currency wallet, public voting/DAO, grant management, volunteer ops. All deferred to roadmap.

## Geography & currency
Mauritius first. Default currency **MUR**. Peach Payments + MCB rails. Locale `en-MU` initially, FR-ready.
